Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Dayline is a free daily 3D racing time-trial game. This page explains what information the game uses and why. We keep it to the minimum the game needs.

What we collect

  • Gameplay data: your lap times, the recorded ghost of your run, the car and paint you used, coins, owned items, settings and achievements.
  • A player ID: a random ID created the first time you play, so your times appear on leaderboards. You do not need an account to play.
  • Optional profile details: a display name you choose, and if you make an account, a username and a password. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text.
  • CrazyGames account (if you sign in there): your CrazyGames user ID and username, received through the CrazyGames SDK, used only to link your progress to your account.
  • Technical data: like any website, our server receives your IP address and browser details in normal request logs. We do not use them to identify you.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, location or payment details, and we do not run our own advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To run the game: daily tracks, leaderboards, ghosts, weekly cups, multiplayer rooms and saving your progress.
  • To prevent cheating and keep the leaderboards fair.
  • To fix problems and improve the game.

What other people can see

Your display name, lap times, car, paint, ghost replays and public stats appear on leaderboards and in rooms. Do not use a name that identifies you if you do not want it shown.

Storage

Progress is saved in your browser (local storage) and, when you are online, on our server (hosted with Supabase). Clearing your browser data removes the local copy; guests without an account may lose progress that way.

Advertising and third parties

When Dayline is played on CrazyGames, ads are provided by CrazyGames through its SDK and are governed by the CrazyGames privacy policy. We do not receive personal data from those ads. On other sites the game may offer rewarded ads from an ad provider under that provider’s policy. Google sign-in, where offered, shares only an account identifier and first name with us.

Children

Dayline is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your player record is active. To have your data deleted, contact us with your player name or username and we will remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top.

Contact
